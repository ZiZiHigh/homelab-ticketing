# Sample tickets

Three simulated tickets written in the format I use on the desk. They are
training material, not customer data: the people and the details are invented,
and the systems are the ones in this lab.

The point is the shape of the work, not the commands:

- what the user said, in their words, before anyone translated it;
- what I checked, in the order I checked it;
- what the evidence showed;
- what I changed, and how I verified it;
- what I would do differently.

Two of the ticket numbers match the queue in the screenshots
(`471582` and `267572`). `918204` is an extra sample, so the third topic has a
written example too.

| Ticket | Topic | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| [471582](471582-printer-offline.md) | Printer does not print | Scope, queues and a DHCP address that moved |
| [267572](267572-account-locked.md) | Cannot log in | Lockout, expired password and cached credentials at home |
| [918204](918204-slow-network.md) | Everything is slow | Ping by address versus by name, and stale VPN DNS |
